### 🚀 About Me

- 🤖 **AI Products:** Built *FutureMe AI*, a career & study guidance platform for Thai students on the principle **"Rules decide. AI explains."**: a transparent scoring engine makes the decision, and the LLM interviews and explains.
- 🔎 **Data & Forensics:** Investigated a 1.4 GB / 21M-line access log with `awk` / `grep` / `sed` to trace a 19-IP botnet, then presented the findings in an offline dashboard.
- 🍓 **Hardware & Home Lab:** Running a Raspberry Pi 5 lab with Python services for dashboards and market monitoring.
- 🌍 **Open Source:** Contributor to [jason5ng32/MyIP](https://github.com/jason5ng32/MyIP) (⭐ 12k) and [meteor/meteor](https://github.com/meteor/meteor) (⭐ 44k).
